Sea smoke drifted over the harbor on January 12. The water was black glass, the air thin and salty. June Tam stood on the dock and watched the vapor rise from the lake. June had not expected to feel anything about the smoke. She felt something about the smoke.

I placed that scene early in *Ice Out* to show how the lake can hide and reveal at once. The harbor garden behind the restaurant watches the same smoke each year. The moment is a pause before the thaw. The book is available on my site.
