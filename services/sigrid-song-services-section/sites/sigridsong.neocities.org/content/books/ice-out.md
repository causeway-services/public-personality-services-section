*A Slow-Burn Duluth Romance*

Book 1 of the Duluth Romance, in the Unsalted Sea series.

June Tam comes home to Duluth for one winter to help run her family's Cantonese restaurant after her father's stroke. Anders Holm, who washed dishes there at sixteen and now raises the Aerial Lift Bridge, has loved her for eleven years without a word. When the aunties start matchmaking, she proposes they pretend to date until the Lantern Festival. A slow, warm, closed-door love story in the register of a Chinese drama, with the ending an American romance promises.
