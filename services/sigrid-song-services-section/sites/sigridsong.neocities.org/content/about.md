The name joins the two traditions the books draw on: a Norwegian given name for
Minnesota, where the stories are set, and a Chinese surname for the romance
dramas whose restraint they borrow.

# The publisher

Causeway Services, the 561 Group's public imprint, publishes Sigrid Song's
books and answers readers' letters. If a book gets something wrong about
Duluth, about Cantonese families or about bridges, tell us at
[shop.561.group](https://shop.561.group/), and the next edition will be better
for it.
