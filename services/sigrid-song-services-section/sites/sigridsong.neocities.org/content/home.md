Sigrid Song writes slow, warm love stories set on the shore of Lake Superior.
They are told in the register of the Chinese romance drama: love shown in small
attentions before anyone says it, slow to arrive and years deep. They end the
way an American romance promises, out loud and never in doubt.

The first is [Ice Out](/books/ice-out/). June Tam comes home to Duluth for
one winter to help run her family's Cantonese restaurant, and Anders Holm, who
raises the Aerial Lift Bridge, has loved her for eleven years without a word.
It opens the Duluth Romance, part of the Unsalted Sea series.

[About Sigrid Song](/about/) says where her name comes from.
