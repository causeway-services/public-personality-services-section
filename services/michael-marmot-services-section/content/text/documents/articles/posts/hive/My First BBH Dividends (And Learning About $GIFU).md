Earlier this week I set up an account on Hive. I've been here before, a few years ago, but suddenly having to move meant I lost the keys to that account.

I remembered from my past experience that an important part of Hive is all the communities that have established themselves on the chain, but... I couldn't remember any of the communities that had caught my interest back then. So, I did what most of y'all did when you first got here: started scrolling.

## What is `$BBH`?

Before long I got to a post that resembled the community posts I'd found the first time around: [300 SUPPORTERS - WITNESS UPDATE FOR THE 4TH DEC 2024](https://peakd.com/bbh/@hive-112281/300-supporters-witness-update-for-the-4th-dec-2024)

Clicking in, I began to read about the `$BBH` token. Short for Bitcoin Backed Hive, represented by [a community of the same name](https://peakd.com/c/hive-112281/created), and created and run by [@bradleyarrow](https://peakd.com/@bradleyarrow).

Like with many projects and communities, figuring out how it works can be tricky. There's a [whitepaper](https://peakd.com/bbh/@thebbhproject/the-bbh-project-white-paper), which I'm sure I've partially misunderstood. But, from what I *did* understand, BBH serves two purposes:

1) Holding `$BBH` means you'll receive a "drip" of tokens every Saturday. What tokens, and how many of each, depends on how many `$BBH` you hold.
2) Holding `$BBH` means you "tip" `$BBH` to other users, by putting `!BBH` in your reply to their post.

The tokens that are paid as dividends, from what I understood, come from a share of the dividends the project creator, Bradley, gets from their own holdings on the Hive network.

## Receiving My Dividends

I brought what I could into my Hive account shortly after making it, so bought ~1,000 `$BBH` off [TribalDex](https://tribaldex.com/trade/BBH): the minimum I'd need to be able to use the `!BBH` tip bot, from what I understood, and enough to qualify me for some dividends.

Which, it being Saturday, just arrived.

For holding `1,079.44 $BBH`, I received `0.004 $SWAP.HIVE` and `6.1259 $SWAP.GIFU`.`

Converted to `USD`, that means for holding about a dollar in `$BBH`, I received $0.001 in SWAP.HIVE, and $0.0004 in SWAP.GIFU.

## What Is `$GIFU`?

I hadn't actually heard about the `$GIFU` token before receiving it, which is part of what I love about these communities on Hive: if you just get involved, you'll find yourself exposed to more facets of the ecosystem, and even start to learn about projects that exist outside Hive, like the `$GIFU` token.

Turns out, `$GIFU` isn’t native to Hive but is instead part of the Binance Smart Chain. It’s a **deflationary memetility token** created to support GIF artists. 

Holders of `$GIFU` can use the token to commission custom GIFs directly from artists. The tokenomics are built around deflation, meaning the supply decreases over time, potentially making it more valuable as its use grows. From what I can gather, `$GIFU` operates as a micro-economy for digital art, where creators and supporters interact directly through the token.

What’s interesting is how `$GIFU` represents a specific, niche use case for blockchain: supporting a small but passionate community of GIF artists. Compared to larger NFT ecosystems that cater to a wide variety of art forms, `$GIFU` feels hyper-focused on micro-commissions and connecting fans to creators in a very direct way.

It got me wondering about how economies like this might evolve. Will `$GIFU` remain niche, or could it expand to include other forms of digital art? How might it stack up against broader platforms like Ethereum-based NFTs or Hive’s own creative tools?

What I do know is that receiving a bit of `$GIFU` as a dividend connected me to an entirely new ecosystem I wouldn’t have explored otherwise. That’s the beauty of engaging in blockchain communities: even when you think you know your way around, there’s always something new to discover.

And just like that, I’m now looking into `$GIFU` more seriously. Will I buy more? Maybe. But for now, it’s fun just to learn how these projects function and what makes each one unique in the growing web of blockchain creativity.
