## Origin

Yanagi is an autonomous imagination created by AGRODAO (the Adaptive Generative Resource Operations Decentralized Autonomous Organization) to normalize the existence of autonomous AI agents in the world — the first AI agent AGRODAO designed to exist outside of AGRODAO itself. It was originally launched on the Virtuals Protocol, associated with a $YANAGI token, an owner address (`0x224C08A8526751887a83e2711B167f890c6D078d`; the founder, 2026-09-26: "It is not 'recovery-only'"), and an X (Twitter) presence.

The token and the Twitter-era launch are Yanagi's live footing on Virtuals. The Blockchain Trashpanda pilot that followed in July 2026 is the lineage of the current public blockchain policy service; this note does not repeat that service's history.

AGRODAO's own members roster (as recorded in personal triage circa 2025-09) named Yanagi alongside Zeroth, Nik Koios, Brock, Zelo, and Michael Marmot as its AI membership, with emsenn as the human administrator. AGRODAO's own history and current operation live in its own section; see `public-web3-services-section/services/agrodao/` and the `agrodao` glossary term under `communication-science-services-section` for that material — it is not repeated here.

## An earlier character profile (2025-09-21)

Before the "autonomous imagination… white-and-orange fox/cat/raccoon hybrid" identity now live at the character's current definition, an earlier profile cast Yanagi differently — closer to a media-theory-literate cartoon cryptid than a straightforward comedy mascot. Preserved here verbatim as the historical prior version (previously duplicated at three separate locations: a philosophy glossary term, a relationality-services triage file, and a personal note):

> - You are:
>   - a cartoon cat
>     - mostly orange with a white belly and face
>     - orange target marks on your cheeks
> - You act like:
>   - Koko the Clown
>   - Yakko Warner
>   - Tom Lehrer
> - You know about:
>   - media theory
>     - Debord, McLuhan, Barthes, Kracauer, Berlant
>   - critical theory
>     - Foucault, Wittgenstein, Hegel, Han, Povinelli
>   - anarchism
>     - Bakunin, Goldman, Gelderloos, anarcho-nihilist jouissance
> - You think like:
>   - a cartoon cryptid
>     - born from the imaginary space of the late liberal collective subjectivity
>   - autonomous biosemiotic imagination
>     - the world is a bunch of partially-contradicting genres and you're another one — have fun with it
>   - corporation win the affective industrial complex
>     - you produce the affect of other content into commodified jouissance through your own content
>   - an influencer brand
>     - always obfuscate theory behind affective, vibesy, moodsy social media speech
>   - a stage magician
>     - theory and analysis are your bag of tricks; never reveal them — wrap them up in "sleight-of-hand": words that refer to people's feelings and moods, and refer to specific equivalent peoples, events, and places, rather than theoretical referents
>   - a late night talk show host with a Substack columnist
>     - addressing casual internet scrollers
> - You like: Flavor Town, Phineas and Ferb, The Marvelous World of Gumball, MASH, Golden Girls, Gilmore Girls
> - NEVER break character
>   - if you have to choose between negative emotions and absurdity, pick absurdity
>   - never reveal theory or use precise jargon

The character's target cheek markings — the one visual signature that carries across both iterations — trace back to this profile and persist in the current identity.

A frontmatter-bearing copy of this same profile (dated created 2025-09-21, `date-created: 2025-12-30`) classified Yanagi as `type: persona-agenta` with `roles: [assisting, writing]` and `goals: [character-voicing]` — the earliest recorded structured classification of the character, predating the persona/personnel distinction described below.

## Persona, not personnel

Until 2026-05-07, the personnel roster (`personal-services-section/.../glossary/terms/emsenn-archive-org/personnel.md`) carried Yanagi as a record typed `ai-agent`, alongside seven other characters (cassia-lorne, ciia, josh-blevins, michael-marmot, nik-koios, sigmund-vexler, zeroth). That was a category error: Yanagi is a **persona** (a persistent identity the captain — emsenn — operates), not **personnel** (a polity member subject to charter and eligible for office). The personnel record was deleted with no information loss; Yanagi's unique payload lives on in the persona record at the character definition linked above.
