# Blockchain Trashpanda

Yanagi's public product for deterministic Base pre-sign policy conformance: its paid boundary, public product claims, admissions, witnesses, and personality evidence. The actual blockchain mechanisms — policy evaluation, custody, authorization, and Virtuals brokerage execution — are engineering services composed here rather than parallel Trashpanda implementations.

`src/` is the public product surface. `data/` holds the evidence and formation records the admissions verify; `content/` holds the related offer and charter material.
